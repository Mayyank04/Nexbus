## 3. Data Lineage & Missingness Policy

1. **Non-Substitution Rule:** When live telemetry or sensor feeds are missing or unauthenticated, NEXBUS sets `status: 'UNAVAILABLE'` or `status: 'AUTH_REQUIRED'`. Missing ground truth observations are **never** populated with synthetic estimates.
2. **Privacy Redaction:** Raw GPS latitude/longitude are truncated to 4 decimal places before storage; vehicle license plates are redacted (`DL-1PD-****`); observer IDs are pseudonymized with salted HMAC-SHA256 digests.
