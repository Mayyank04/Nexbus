# NEXBUS — PASSENGER PRODUCT & ACCESSIBILITY GUIDE
**Document Reference:** NEXBUS-COMMUTER-GUIDE-V1  
**Target Route:** `/journey`  
**Audience:** Everyday Delhi Transit Commuters, Students, and Daily Bus Passengers  
**Design Principle:** Clean, human-centered, zero-jargon transit guidance with honest confidence intervals  

---

## 1. Recommendation Category Tiers

When a commuter selects any origin and destination across the National Capital Territory of Delhi, NEXBUS evaluates all available transit corridors and presents three distinct, non-overlapping Pareto options:

1. **`FASTEST` Tier:**
   - Minimizes total journey duration by prioritizing rapid express corridors (e.g. Ring Road Rapid, Outer Ring Road Flyovers).
   - Typically features 0 to 1 transfers.
   - Ideal for time-sensitive office workers and morning peak commuters.
2. **`BALANCED` Tier:**
   - Balances journey duration, seat availability, and transfer walking distances.
   - Recommended for general daytime travel when extreme speed is not critical.
3. **`COMFORT FIRST` Tier:**
   - Maximizes seating probability ($\ge 75\%$) and targets low-crowding buses.
   - Prioritizes air-conditioned low-floor electric buses with step-free boarding.
   - Recommended for elderly travelers, passengers carrying luggage, or those commuting during extreme summer/pollution episodes.

---

## 2. Accessible Journey Guidance

In compliance with Phase 6 accessibility directives, every route option now surfaces:
- **Low-Floor / Step-Free Indicators:** Verified DTC electric buses equipped with pneumatic kneeling systems and wheelchair boarding ramps.
- **Underpass & Transfer Walking Guidance:** Explicit notes regarding whether metro-to-bus interchange involves step-free escalators or surface zebra crossings.
- **Headway & Frequency Indicators:** Informs commuters of expected bus arrival intervals (~5–7 min during rush hours).

---

## 3. Epistemological Transparency for Commuters

> [!NOTE]
> **No Fake Live Telemetry Policy**  
> NEXBUS explicitly marks crowding levels (`Low`, `Moderate`, `High`) as statistical model estimates calibrated from historical DTC transaction records. Commuters are never deceptively told that an AI is viewing real-time CCTV feeds inside every bus.
