## 2. Key Competitive Differentiators

1. **Anti-Slop Architectural Dignity:** Unlike generic hackathon entries covered in glowing cyan boxes, gradient text, and dark-navy cards, NEXBUS respects the typography and cartography of national transport monographs.
2. **True Dual-Mode Digital Twin:** Bridges the gap between complex municipal GIS modeling and child-friendly civic education.
3. **Multi-Criteria Itinerary Intelligence:** Balances journey time, transfer physical friction (IFI), wheelchair accessibility, and crowding.
4. **Empirical Scientific Traceability:** Every metric is categorized as Observed, Estimated, Predicted, or Simulated. Zero fabricated or hallucinated numbers.
5. **Cross-Platform Ready:** Operates as a responsive Web PWA, Android/iOS application via Capacitor, and macOS/Windows desktop binary via Tauri.
