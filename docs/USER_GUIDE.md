# NEXBUS — Commuter User Guide

Welcome to NEXBUS, Delhi's intelligent public transit companion. This guide explains how to find the best bus journey across the National Capital Territory.

---

## 1. Finding Your Journey (`/journey`)

1. **Origin & Destination:**
   - Click on the **FROM (ORIGIN)** field to open the searchable location autocomplete.
   - Type any stop name (e.g. *AIIMS*, *Connaught Place*, *Kashmere Gate*, *Dhaula Kuan*, *Nehru Place*) or landmark.
   - Matching bus stops, Hindi names, administrative zones, and Metro interchange lines appear automatically.
   - Use the round directional arrow button to swap origin and destination.

2. **Selecting Departure Time:**
   - The planner defaults to current time. Adjust the **DEPARTURE TIME** input to see predicted crowding conditions during morning peak (08:30) or evening peak (18:00).

3. **Popular Trip Chips:**
   - Tap any quick trip chip (e.g. *AIIMS → Connaught Place*, *Kashmere Gate → Nehru Place*) for one-tap routing.

---

## 2. Choosing Between the 3 Route Options

NEXBUS computes Pareto-optimal choices tailored to different commuter priorities:

| Route Tier | Badge | When to Choose | Key Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fastest Option** | `Speed Optimized` | You have a strict schedule and want minimal total minutes. | Direct arterial express corridors with flyovers. |
| **Balanced Option** | `Recommended Choice` | You want the best balance between speed, transfers, and wait times. | Frequent headways (5–6 min) with minimal platform delay. |
| **Less Crowded Option** | `Comfort First` | You want a guaranteed seat or prefer an air-conditioned electric bus. | Avoids crush-load segments; 88%+ seat availability. |

---

## 3. Reading Journey Segments

Each route option displays:
- **Walking Connections:** Distance in meters and walking minutes from curb to platform.
- **Boarding Instructions:** Bus route number (e.g. `Route 502 EXP`, `Ring Sewa TMS (+)`), vehicle fleet type (e.g. *Electric AC 12m Low Floor*), and number of intermediate stops.
- **Metro Interchange Guidance:** Identified Metro connection nodes (e.g. *Rajiv Chowk Yellow/Blue Line*, *AIIMS Yellow Line*).
